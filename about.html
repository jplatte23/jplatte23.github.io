About Me

Hi, I’m Jason, a recent USC Computer Science Games graduate focused on gameplay engineering.

I’ve worked across Unity and Unreal Engine on player-facing systems including multiplayer mechanics, AI behavior, physics, UI, progression, combat, and game feel. I’m currently developing Wielderless, a co-op action roguelite prototype in Unreal Engine where players are cursed weapons that possess temporary bodies.

Games were a big part of my upbringing, especially Halo: Reach and Borderlands 2, and so were robotics and esports. Through VEX and FIRST Robotics, I got hooked on solving technical problems with a team. Esports taught me how much player experience depends on communication, readable systems, and decisions under pressure.

That mix of games, engineering, and teamwork is what pulled me toward gameplay programming. I enjoy solving technical problems that directly affect the player experience, especially in gameplay systems, multiplayer, UI, and adjacent areas like engine programming, simulation, and graphics.
