Lessons Learned

What Summit taught me.

Multiplayer systems need clear ownership boundaries. Separating client responsiveness from server authority helped keep movement and interactions feeling good without making shared state fragile.
Traversal mechanics are also economy mechanics. Stamina costs, item value, and progression rewards all affected how players chose routes and took risks.
Cross-platform design has to be tested, not assumed. PC and mobile controls changed how climbing, lunge timing, and UI flows needed to be tuned.
